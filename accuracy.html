How to read the score

Every prediction earns a score from -100 to +100 points based on how right — or wrong — the call turned out to be. Positive means the call was correct; negative means it wasn't. The size of the number is the size of the win or loss, not just whether it happened: a strong, clearly-correct call scores near +100, a narrow miss scores near 0. Stock and crypto picks are also weighed against the overall market (the S&P 500 or Bitcoin) — a stock only earns credit for beating the market, not just for rising alongside it.

+85 strong, clearly correct call +3 technically right, barely moved -40 market moved firmly the other way

The small letter grade next to each score (A-F) is just a quick summary of that same number.
