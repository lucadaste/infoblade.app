Prediction Markets

Does the market know something the news doesn't?

We pull live questions from Polymarket and run them through the same news-analysis pipeline that powers the daily feed. The result: a real-time signal showing whether current market odds align with, or contradict, what the news is actually saying.

If the crowd is pricing a 72% chance of a rate cut and our news analysis tells a different story, you'll see exactly that tension and why it exists.

In plain English: a prediction market is a live poll with money on the line, its price is the crowd's best guess at the odds of "yes." We check whether the news actually backs that guess up.
